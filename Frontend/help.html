How Upay_cloak works, from a payment to a closed case.

How the system works

1
Score every transaction

Each transfer is scored by a trained machine-learning model (LightGBM) that compares it with the customer's own history. Signals include a new device or place, odd hours, an amount far from the usual, a first-time recipient, what the money is for (for example betting), OTP behaviour (OTP confirmed from another device, repeated OTP requests or failures, two sessions at once, a recent SIM swap) and links to mule wallets. Every signal shows how much it raised or lowered the score.

2
Decide what happens to the money

The score is compared with thresholds the model learned from cost trade-offs (shown at /api/v1/config): below the first threshold the transfer is allowed, above it the customer must verify, higher scores are held for an analyst, and the highest are blocked. Policy rules can raise the action: a rule alone can only ask for extra verification, while a hold or block also needs the model to agree. Wallets in a detected mule ring are frozen.

3
Open a case for risky activity

High and critical transactions, and known scam scenarios, become cases. Each case answers three questions: what happened, why it is risky, and what to do next.

4
Investigate and act

Open a case to see the timeline, the reasons behind the score and the connected wallets. Ask the assistant a question, then choose Hold, Block, Request KYC, Escalate or Mark as false positive. Every action is saved in the audit trail.

5
Warn the customer

The Warning demo shows what a customer sees in the upay app before sending money, next to the scoring behind it.

Alert types

Account takeover
Someone else is using a customer's account, often from a new device.
Mule network
Many accounts pass stolen money to one wallet, then cash out fast.
Scam
A customer is tricked into sending money.
OTP breach
Someone else is using a customer's one-time code, for example confirmed from a different device while a second session is open.
Betting / gambling
Payments to betting services, especially repeated payments with growing stakes in a few hours.
Agent anomaly
An agent's activity is far from what similar agents do.
Structuring
Repeated cash-outs just under the 50,000 BDT reporting limit.
Step-up
An extra check, such as a PIN, before the money moves.

About this demo

Data
All customers, wallets and transactions are synthetic, so accuracy numbers are optimistic.
PIN
In the Warning demo, the step-up PIN is 1234.
Settings
Your name, feed speed and demo language are saved in this browser only.
Backend
Every score comes from the trained model on the server. The dashboard replays held-out transactions as a live feed, and the Warning demo scores real transactions from that data (you can edit the amount and recipient to see the score change).
